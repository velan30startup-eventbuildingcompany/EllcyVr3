About Our Decoration Service

ELLCY Decoration transforms any venue — your Mahal, hall or home — into a breathtaking setting. From elegant minimal backdrops and lush floral arches to dramatic LED-lit stage setups, our team handles every detail with precision and creativity.

Whether it's an intimate birthday or a grand wedding reception, we offer thoughtfully designed packages to suit every budget — each delivering the same commitment to beauty and quality that our clients trust.

Tip: When you book, you can add your Mahal / venue name and upload up to 4 photos of your event location so our decorators can plan the design in advance.
