ELLCY's Nadhaswaram & Thavil ensemble brings the sacred sound of traditional South Indian classical music to your celebrations. The melodious Nadhaswaram, paired with the powerful Thavil percussion, creates an auspicious atmosphere that blesses every milestone moment.

Our master musicians are seasoned performers with decades of experience at weddings, temple festivals, and grand processions across Chennai and Tamil Nadu. From intimate 2-member reception duos to majestic 12-member wedding ensembles, every performance is delivered with devotion and precision.
