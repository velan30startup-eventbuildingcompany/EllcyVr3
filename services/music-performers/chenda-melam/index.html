Chenda Melam is a powerful traditional percussion ensemble from Kerala, known for its thunderous rhythms and vibrant energy. It brings divine intensity and cultural grandeur to festivals, weddings and temple celebrations.

Experience the electrifying beats of Chenda Melam — a centuries-old art form of Kerala. Performed by skilled artists using chenda drums, kombu, elathalam and kuzhal, this ensemble creates an atmosphere that resonates with emotion, culture and celebration, leaving every guest awestruck.
